PersonalMentor is an Android app with two modes: Task Mode (an assistant that manages your to-dos and reminders) and Mentor Mode (an assistant that answers questions, optionally using documents you add). This policy explains what happens to your data.

1. What stays on your device

Your chat history, tasks, reminders, the documents you add to the knowledge base (and their searchable index) and your settings are stored only on your device. We have no user accounts and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, advertising ID or analytics.

2. What is sent to our server and to the AI provider

To produce answers, the app sends the following over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our proxy server, which forwards it to an AI service provider (REPLACE_PROVIDER_NAME) acting on our behalf:

DataWhenWhy
Your messages and the recent conversationEvery time you send a messageTo generate the reply
Task titles, notes and timesWhen the Task Mode assistant reads or changes your tasksSo it can answer about and manage your tasks
Text of documents you add (in small passages)When a document is indexed, and when a passage is relevant to your questionTo search your documents and answer from them
A reported reply, the reason and your optional noteOnly when you tap “Report” on a replyTo review unsafe or wrong AI output

Our server does not store your messages or documents; it only passes them on. It may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and abuse prevention. The AI provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy and may retain it for a limited period (for example for abuse monitoring). Reports are kept as long as needed to improve safety and then deleted.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information (such as passwords, financial account numbers or health records) in chats or documents.

3. AI-generated content

Replies are generated by an AI and can be wrong or inappropriate. Use the “Report” button under a reply to flag it; do not rely on replies for medical, legal or financial decisions.

4. Permissions

  • Internet – to reach the AI service.
  • Notifications – to show reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders – so reminders arrive at the exact time (optional).
  • Run at startup – to re-arm reminders after the phone restarts.

5. Your choices and deleting data

You can delete a conversation (“clear chat”), remove documents from the knowledge base, delete tasks, or uninstall the app, which removes all data stored on your device. To ask us to delete a report you sent, or any other question about your data, write to REPLACE_CONTACT_EMAIL.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
